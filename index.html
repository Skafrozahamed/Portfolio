About Me

I am an Electronics & Communication Engineering graduate with hands-on industrial maintenance experience in solar manufacturing and Indian Railways. Currently working as a Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET) - Maintenance at Gautam Solar Pvt. Ltd., Midnapore, West Bengal, India.

My expertise lies in preventive and breakdown maintenance, electrical fault diagnosis, machine inspection, and control systems. I work extensively with A050k Solar Cell Stringer Machines in PV module production, performing diagnostics and rectifying electrical, sensor, soldering, alignment, and control faults.

I have proven ability to reduce downtime, maintain quality standards, and support continuous plant operations. I inspect heaters, soldering units, conveyors, and control panels while following strict safety procedures and maintenance checklists.

I bring a strong troubleshooting mindset with expertise in C, C++, Python, and IoT systems. I'm passionate about building embedded systems projects using ESP32 and integrating hardware with cloud platforms like ThingSpeak for real-time monitoring.
